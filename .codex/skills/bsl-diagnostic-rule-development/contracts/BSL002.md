# BSL002 MethodSize Contract

## Rule Identity

- Code: BSL002
- BSLLS compatible name: MethodSize
- Public name/i18n key: BSL002 / MethodSize
- Default message/i18n key: RULE_MESSAGES_RU["BSL002"]
- Description/i18n key: RULE_METADATA["BSL002"].description
- Severity: WARNING in current emitted diagnostics; RULE_METADATA keeps `ERROR`.
- Parameters/defaults: `maxMethodSize` / `max_proc_lines`, default `200`.
- Rule status: verified

## Standard And Product Decision

- Standard or product policy: large procedures/functions should be decomposed into smaller methods.
- Why this diagnostic exists: oversized methods are difficult to read, review, test, and safely modify.
- Non-goals: cognitive complexity, cyclomatic complexity, excessive returns, or code-block emptiness; those are owned by separate rules.
- Keep/drop decision: keep as a BSLLS-compatible size/readability diagnostic.

## Semantic Oracle

- Checked language fact: a procedure/function body exceeds the configured maximum method size.
- Must diagnose when: the effective body line span of a parsed procedure/function is greater than `maxMethodSize`.
- Must not diagnose when: the body is at or below the limit, the method fact is unavailable, or only comments/blank lines extend the physical span.
- Diagnostic anchor token/range: procedure/function name in the declaration line.
- Quick-fix target, if any: extract coherent body fragments into helper methods.

## CST Or Domain Fact Model

- Parser/tree source: document procedure/function facts from the parsed module model.
- CST/domain object types: `ProcedureModel`/`ProcInfo` with start/end indices and parsed parameters/body.
- Extracted attributes: method start/end lines, first/last non-empty non-comment body line, configured limit, name span, and severity.
- Parse-error behavior: skip uncertain procedure containment when procedure facts are unavailable; BSL001 owns syntax errors.
- Textual/regex behavior, only if genuinely textual: only name-span lookup and comment/blank body-line filtering are textual; method ownership comes from procedure facts.

## Examples

### Invalid Examples

1. A procedure with 201 executable body lines under the default `maxMethodSize=200`.
2. A function with 51 executable body lines when `maxMethodSize=50`.
3. A long method whose body is extended by ordinary statements rather than comments.

### Valid Negative Twins

1. A procedure with 200 executable body lines under the default limit.
2. A method with many trailing comments but an executable body below the configured limit.
3. A short method that is complex; complexity belongs to BSL011/BSL019 instead.

### Edge Examples

- Comments: ignored for first/last effective body line selection.
- Strings: counted as source lines only when they are inside the effective method body span.
- Preprocessor: ordinary body lines contribute when inside the effective span.
- Multiline: multiline statements count by physical body lines.
- Nested constructs: included in the owning method body length.
- BSL/SDBL variants, if relevant: query text is not a separate target.
